# Convergence of G toward Q (χ₂₉)

| μ | L | λ0 G/Q | ||G−Q||_F/||Q||_F |
|---|---|---------|-------------------|
| 11 | 2.40 | 0.929 | 0.065 |
| 22 | 3.09 | 0.882 | 0.054 |
| 38 | 3.64 | 0.826 | 0.041 |

Two different stories.

**Bulk (Frobenius).** Relative error falls. Slope vs μ
steepens (−0.27 then −0.50). A naïve μ^{−1/2} from
the last segment would put 1 % near μ∼600. Three
points do not make that a law. The zeros list is
capped at T=320; beyond μ∼50 the Gram band outruns
the list and the comparison dies like χ₅.

**Ground state (λ0).** G/Q moves *away* from 1.
The desert mode is exactly where a missing high
zero or a prime tail weighs most. Frobenius is
dominated by the large eigenvalues (the pair and
the bulk), which already agree.

So G approaches Q as matrices, not as bottom
eigenvalues. That is why |G−Q|<λ_min(Q) can hold
in F-norm pieces while the RH-grade bound on the
off-line remainder still fails: λ_min itself is
e^{−O(L)}.
