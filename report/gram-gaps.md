# Gaps of the Gram

Here: ratio λ1/λ0 of the zero-side Gram, not
Gram *points* θ(t)=nπ.

## Scaling

When the desert exists, log(gap) grows with μ
(more Slepian isolation of the ground state).
When γ1 ≲ 1, the gap stays O(1) (Δ⊗χ₅).
When γ1 is huge and N exceeds the number of
zeros in band, the gap is undefined (INDEF).

| object | γ1 | gap(22) | gap(38) | log-slope /16 |
|--------|----|---------|---------|----------------|
| 11a1 | 6.36 | 1.2e5 | 6e5 | 0.10 |
| Δ⊗χ₄ | 2.30 | 2.2e2 | 2.4e4 | 0.29 |
| χ₂₉ | ~1.8 | 3e2 | 2e4 | 0.26 |
| Sym² E₁₁ | 3.90 | 6e2 | 3e3 | 0.10 |
| E₁₁⊗χ₅ | 2.35 | 40 | 1e2 | 0.06 |
| Δ⊗χ₅ | 0.89 | 2.2 | 5 | 0.05 |
| maass3 μ=8 | 2.90 | 1.4e4 | — | window not comparable |

The slope of log(gap) is not s_hat (that is
Δell/Δμ). It is a second observable: how fast
the first excited state peels off.

## What a small gap means

λ1 ≈ λ0: two directions equally cheap. N_eff
then exceeds 2. Δ⊗χ₅ is that regime. It is not
a failure of positivity; it is a failure of
*isolation*. Lemma 2 bounds λ0, not λ1/λ0.
