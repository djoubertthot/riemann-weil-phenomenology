# Freeze

Weil explicit formula on the cosine window of length
L = log μ:

    ∑_ρ ĥ(g)(ρ) = arch(g) − ∑_{n<μ} (Λ χ)(n)/√n  g(log n)

g = f ★ f̃, supp g ⊂ (−L,L).
Under RH the left side is the Gram of the hats and
equals Q. Unconditionally the difference is the
off-line zeros. A bound |Q−Gram| < λ_min(Gram)
would be RH. We do not have it.

## What is proved

Slepian on one desert I: ell_Gram ≤ π τ |I| + log(1/A)
once Iᶜ samples PW_τ. Beurling D⁻ does not see I.
Landau–Widom counts eigenvalues of χ_E P χ_E, not Q.

## What is measured

Q > 0 on Dirichlet windows we ran (χ, μ=2…74).
GL2 Q is positive and flat. Δ Q is negative (wrong Γ).
Gram is isolated when γ1 ≳ 2 and the zero list fills
the band; INDEF when it does not (ζ, Δ, χ₅ at large μ).

of the desert. It is not a proof of RH.
