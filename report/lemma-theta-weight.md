# Spectral weight on the desert is not the source of y e^y

Autocorrelation of e^{−αω} 1_{[0,γ₁]} (α = 0.5 … 3, including
the measured 1.34) stays O(10⁻¹) at y = 2.16 and the ratio
(−ln Θ)/(y e^y) *falls* toward 0.1. No oscillation at these α,
but also no drop to 10⁻¹³.

exp(−α ω log(ω+1)) is the same: r ≲ 0.35 and falling.

A density on the frequency axis, even super-exponential, is a
Fourier transform of a Lorentzian / sub-exponential in y.
It cannot produce Θ_Q(2.2) ∼ 10⁻¹³.

The y e^y envelope is forced by the **time limit**:
Θ_v(L) = 0 identically in the hat basis, Paley–Wiener of
bandwidth L. The desert only sets the constant C and
kills the sinc oscillations.

Lemma refined: φ(y) interpolates y² (bulk Gaussian of a
concentrated Slepian) and y e^y (edge, vanishing at L).
C comes from s/L, not from α.
