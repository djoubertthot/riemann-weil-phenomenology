# Tail effects

## Li λ_n (ζ, T=319)

raw tail / zeros ≈ 21–22 % for n=1…8.
The fraction does not grow with n on this
range. After scale 0.55 it is ~12 % of λ_n.

Without the tail, λ1 is 10 % low vs the
closed form. With unscaled Weyl it is 8 %
high. Sign never flips.

## Gram χ₂₉ μ=11

Zeros only to ω≈69: λ0=0.281 (7 % below Q).
T=320: 0.298 (1.6 % below).
Weyl tail s=0.65: hits 0.303.

Same object: missing high t in a positive
kernel. Adding a smooth tail moves the
number toward the explicit-formula side
and cannot cancel λ0.

## What a tail cannot do

Create a negative λ_n or a negative λ0(Q).
That would take an off-line zero, not an
incomplete list.
