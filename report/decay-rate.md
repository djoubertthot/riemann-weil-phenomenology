# Decay rates — χ₂₉ residual

| μ | L | σ₀ | σ₁ | ||D||_F | ||Q||_F | Frel |
|---|---|-----|-----|---------|---------|------|
| 11 | 2.40 | 1.615 | 0.058 | 1.616 | 24.9 | 0.065 |
| 22 | 3.09 | 1.703 | 0.040 | 1.703 | 31.5 | 0.054 |
| 38 | 3.64 | 1.880 | 0.051 | 1.881 | 46.3 | 0.041 |

σ₀ **grows** (slowly). The relative error falls
because ||Q|| grows faster (~ L or a bit more).

Frel slopes vs μ: −0.27 then −0.50.
vs L: Δlog Frel / ΔL = −0.27 over [2.40,3.64]
≈ e^{−0.27 L} would be too slow to beat e^{−c L}
on λ0.

σ₁ stays O(0.05), no collapse of the second
channel. Rank-1 remains (σ₀ ≫ σ₁).

The decay that matters for RH (ε / λ_min) goes
the wrong way: λ_min(Q) at μ=38 is 7e-6,
||D|| is 1.88. The residual is 10⁵ times larger
than the ground state. Frobenius convergence
does not help the bound |Q−G|<λ_min.
