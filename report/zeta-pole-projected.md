# Pole mode projected out of Q_ζ

POLE is rank 1 (one large eigenvalue, rest 0).
Drop that direction, look at Q on the complement.

| μ | overlap(v0, pole) | λ_min raw | λ_min ⊥ pole |
|---|-------------------|-----------|----------------|
| 2 | 0.89 | 0.0015 | **0.551** |
| 4 | 0.69 | ~0 | 0.0049 |

μ=2: the singularity was the pole. After the
projection Q is healthy and O(1), like χ₄ at
μ=2 (Q00=0.51). T₂ still does not enter.

μ=4: projecting one pole direction is not
enough. A second small eigenvalue (~5·10^{-3})
remains — POLE+ARCH still cancel on another
combination as L grows. Two-dimensional
projection of POLE does nothing extra
(rank POLE = 1).

Gram still does not apply (desert 14).
The projected Q at μ=2 is the analogue of
the compact-class Q00, including the pole
handling that χ does not need.
