Still short of 3. The third mode is on
and growing; the fourth is not
(v₃ ≈ −0.07, v_{n≥4} < 10⁻²). χ₃ is
leaving two-mode at a rate of about
+0.02 N_eff per unit of log µ. Reaching
N_eff=3 at this slope wants µ ∼ 10²–10³
and dps well above 30 — a server job,
not another 22-digit scan.
