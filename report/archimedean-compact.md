# Compact archimedean form

On the window, the Γ′/Γ piece of the explicit
formula is an integral of θ_nm(y) against a
smooth kernel on [0, L] only — no tail.
That is the matrix ARCH in spectro_zeta
(and the D2-panel in scan_s).

ζ, μ=2, after the pole mode:

    mode 1: POLE +0.22, ARCH +0.45, T2 ≈ 0
            λ = 0.665

Almost pure ARCH. T2 vanishes (θ(log 2)=0).
The same number as Q00(χ₄)=0.51 and
Q00(χ₅)=0.39 at μ=2: one quadrature of
the completed Γ-factor on a segment of
length log 2.

Properties:

- positive on the complement of the pole
  (ζ) or on the whole space (χ, no pole);
- O(1), independent of the zeros;
- does not dive when μ grows *until* the
  prime sum and the desert start to bite
  (χ₅ already at μ=3; ζ only much later).

It is not a spectral gap of the zeros and
not Slepian. It is the constant term of
Weil on a tiny interval.
