# Remainder of Q_ζ versus Q(χ)

After dropping the pole kernel of ζ:

| μ | ζ rest λ_min | χ₅ λ0 | χ₄ λ0 |
|---|--------------|-------|-------|
| 2 | 0.665 | 0.325 | 0.434 |
| 3 | 0.702 | 0.044 | 0.070 |
| 4 | 0.594 | 0.0053 | — |

χ feels the first zeros (γ1(χ₅)≈6.6) as soon
as L grows; λ0 drops. ζ rest does not: the
desert is at 14, invisible at μ≤5, and the
pole subspace has been removed by hand.

So the O(1) leftover is the compact-class
archimedean positivity, same order as Q00(χ)
at μ=2, and it does *not* start the Slepian
dive. To see a desert mode on ζ one would
need μ ≳ e^{c·14} or a window whose bandwidth
reaches t=14, i.e. L ≳ 2π n / 14 with n large
enough — and a Gram that is not rank-deficient.
That is μ=22 N=37, already INDEF.

ζ is the wrong L for the G/Q programme.
χ₂₉ remains the one where both sides live.
