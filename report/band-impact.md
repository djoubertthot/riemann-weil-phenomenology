## What the band does

1. λ1(G)≈1.75 is flat in N and T. The
   bulk does not see the cutoff.
2. λ0 *falls* slowly as N grows at fixed T
   (0.306→0.298 at T=320). Extra high-k
   hats have longer tails, so more missing
   mass in the desert mode.
3. The dangerous parameter is **T/ω**.
   Below 1 the Gram is indefinite for
   the wrong reason. At T/ω≳5, λ0 has
   settled to 0.298 (1.6 % below Q).
4. Widening the band without raising T
   makes Weil *look* worse. That is why
   N=24 was paired with T=69 only as a
   first look, and T=320 is the real check.

The tail scale s≈0.65 was fitted at N=24.
Reuse only at the same T/ω order.
