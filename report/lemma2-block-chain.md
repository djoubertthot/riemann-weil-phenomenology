## Picture, corrected

One distinguished 3-plane
(hats 0,1,2, ker ψ(0)). A
bulk T that is O(1) on
ordinary windows and develops
a small joint eigenvalue only
when L and N_B are large.
C of size ~1 couples them.
λ₀ is the Schur of that one
special plane against the bulk.

The exponent lives in how
closely Arch tracks Primes on
ker, then in how C projects
that plane into T⁻¹. Not in
a ladder of identical blocks.
