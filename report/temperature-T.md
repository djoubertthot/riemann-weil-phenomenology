## Two regimes

T < ω : Gram is rank-deficient. λ0=0.
Not a physical desert — too few zeros
for 25 modes. Cold, frozen.

T > ω : **Q − λ0 ∼ 1.40 / T**.
d·T is flat from 80 to 320. Not 1/T²
(that would be hats without Weyl log).
A 1/T tail is ∫_T^∞ (log t)/t² dt after
the leading 1/t is cancelled by the
oscillation of the hats, leaving log/T.

## Reading

T is an ultraviolet cutoff on the
critical line. Raising it heats the
desert mode toward Q, at 1.4/T per
unit of height. At T=320 the mode is
1.5 % cold. T=1000 would leave ~0.001
if the law holds — still not RH, just
a smaller truncation.
