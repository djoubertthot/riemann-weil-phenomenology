# Negative modes stay delocalized

⟨x⟩ and σ_x of every λ<0.

N=40..160: centroids in (0.24, 0.72), widths
σ_x ≈ 0.18–0.32. They do not pack at ρ=1
and they do not shrink.

The extra pairs fill the same interval
with more oscillations, like a Fourier
basis of [0,1] — consistent with a
non-trace-class kernel, not a local
defect at one point.
