∑λ²: 6 negatives = 1.19, 3 larges = 1.29, mid+tiny = 0.15.

The ground mode is at x≈0. The **negative** tail sits
in the middle of [0,1] and is a difference of shells
(n=1,2,3 minus a bit of n=0). That is the HS log:
more n resolve more sign-changing modes around x=1/2,
not a peak at ρ=1 on the ground state.
