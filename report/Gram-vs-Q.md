# Gram vs Q on the compact class

Same window as scan_s. Gram = 2 ΦᵀΦ from zeros_chi*_weyl.pkl.

| χ | μ | λ0(Q) | λ0(Gram) | Gram/Q | |G−Q| | < λ0(Q) ? |
|---|---|-------|----------|--------|------|-----------|
| χ₅ | 2 | 0.325 | 0.602 | 1.85 | 0.277 | yes |
| χ₅ | 3 | 0.044 | 0.076 | 1.73 | 0.032 | yes |
| χ₅ | 4 | 0.0053 | 0.0094 | 1.78 | 0.0041 | yes |
| χ₄ | 2 | 0.434 | 0.825 | 1.90 | 0.391 | yes |
| χ₄ | 3 | 0.070 | 0.122 | 1.75 | 0.052 | yes |

Gram/Q ≈ 2 : the explicit 2 in 2ΦᵀΦ. After /2 the
values track (0.30 vs 0.32, 0.038 vs 0.044).

|G−Q| < λ0(Q) holds *numerically* on this class.
It does not prove RH: ĥ at μ=2 has width ~9, zeros
are truncated, the factor 2 is a convention, and
the off-line remainder is not isolated.

What we can say: on μ≤4, Q and Gram/2 agree at
10–15 %. That is the identity with a cutoff error,
not ε < λ_min as a theorem.
