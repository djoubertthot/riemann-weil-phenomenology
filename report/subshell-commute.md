# Commutators and spatial profile

‖[F^{(n)},F^{(m)}]‖_F / (‖Fⁿ‖‖Fᵐ‖) = 0.26–0.58
for n≠m ≤ 4. They do **not** commute.
Shared top mode ≠ simultaneous diagonalisation.

Spatial profile on [0,1] (even cells):

    x     v0    v1    v2    vΣ
    0.03  0.37  0.45  0.53  0.70
    0.23  0.32  0.33  0.28  0.21
    0.53  0.16  0.08  0.02  0.03
    0.93  0.02  0.00  0.00 -0.03

All decay from the origin. Higher n is more
concentrated at 0. v_Σ is the most peaked.
The log HS lives in the many small modes
near ρ=1, not in this ground profile.
