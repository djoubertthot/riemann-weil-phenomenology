# Truncation of the zero list (χ₂₉ μ=11, Q fixed)

Hats have tails past ω=2π(N−1)/L ≈ 63.
Including zeros above ω still changes G.

| T | nz | λ0(G) | Frel | σ₀(G−Q) |
|---|-----|-------|------|---------|
| 20 | 11 | ~0 | 0.90 | 7.44 |
| 50 | 35 | ~0 | 0.55 | 7.34 |
| 63 | 47 | 0.275 | 0.15 | 3.59 |
| 69 | 52 | 0.281 | 0.065 | 1.62 |
| 100 | 82 | 0.289 | 0.037 | 0.92 |
| 200 | 186 | 0.296 | 0.019 | 0.47 |
| 320 | 321 | 0.298 | 0.013 | 0.31 |

Q λ0 = 0.303. At T=320, G/Q = 0.984,
Frel = 1.3 %. The rank-1 channel shrinks
as T grows: it is the missing tail of the
hats, not an off-line zero.

Primes are not truncated (n < μ=11 is the
full support). The error is spectral
truncation on the left-hand side of Weil.
A complete list (or a decaying tail model)
would push Frel below 1 %.
