Drop every raw eigenvalue < 0.05, read the rest.

| μ | # tiny | rest λ_min |

| 2 | 1 | 0.665 |

The cancelled subspace grows with the window
(more cosines fit ĥ(g)(0)+ĥ(g)(1)). What remains
is stable and O(1): the same compact-class
positivity as χ at μ=2–4.

This is not the desert (γ1=14). It is the pole
eating one new direction per increment of μ.
Projecting a single POLE eigenvector is only
correct at μ=2.
