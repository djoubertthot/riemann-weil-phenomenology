Frobenius, p=∞ operator.

    ||D||_1 ≈ ||D||_2 ≈ ||D||_∞ ≈ σ₀.

||D||_1 / ||Q||_1 = 1.67/116 ≈ 1.4 % at μ=11
(same as the trace gap). The 6 % F-relative
and the 22 % operator-relative are the same
σ₀ read against different norms of Q.

No new information in p: once rank-1 is
granted, choosing Schatten is choosing how
you normalise σ₀ by Q, not a different
residual.
