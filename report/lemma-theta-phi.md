# φ, not Θ, is the bump

Cosine reconstruction ψ(t) = v₀/√L + ∑ vₙ √(2/L) cos(ωₙ t)
on [0, L], µ=11, the four-mode v.

ψ is **even about L/2**, a bump in the middle,
edges at 10⁻⁵:

    t/L     ψ           −ln|ψ|
    0.00    8×10⁻⁶      11.74
    0.25    0.064        2.75
    0.50    1.51        −0.41
    0.75    0.064        2.75
    1.00    8×10⁻⁶      11.74

−ln|ψ(0)| = 11.74 = s(ζ) at this window.

Θ_v is the autocorrelation of this bump.
A shift y slides two copies; the overlap
is the product of a tail e^{−s} with a
bulk value — that is where y e^y can live,
as the edge law of a function that is
e^{−s} at 0 and L and order 1 at L/2.

The lemma is now: the ground state of Q
is a mid-window bump with edge e^{−s}.
That is the Slepian of the *time* window,
not of the desert.
