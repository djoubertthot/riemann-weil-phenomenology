## One-zero tent

The hat of the tent on [0,L] at
the first zero:

    (1 − cos(γ₁ L)) / (γ₁² L)

versus the measured |ψ(0)|.

| L | µ | \|ψ(0)\| | tent | 1/(γ₁ √L) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| χ₅ 16 | 3.3e-6 | 7.0e-4 | 9.0e-2 |
| χ₅ 38 | 6.1e-11 | 2.6e-3 | 7.9e-2 |
| χ₃ 80 | 2.2e-24 | 6.3e-3 | 5.9e-2 |
| χ₁₃ 16 | 1.7e-2 | 6.4e-2 | 1.9e-1 |
| χ₃₁ 38 | 3.5e-2 | 3.7e-2 | 2.6e-1 |

The tent is within a factor two
only when |ψ(0)| is not small
(χ₃₁, χ₁₃). On a wide desert it
misses 10⁵–10²¹. The ground state
is not the first Gram term.

## Consequence

There is no elementary function of
(γ₁, L) that tracks |ψ(0)| at
constant C₀ on these eleven
windows. Lemma 2 on the edge is
still the right *statement*
(ell ≈ −2 ln|ψ(0)| is the robust
line). The comparison function
is not Slepian on [0,γ₁], not
Lγ₁, not a single sinc.

A proof has to use the whole
sampling set Γ, or a better
test function than the tent —
the ground state of Q itself,
which is circular.
