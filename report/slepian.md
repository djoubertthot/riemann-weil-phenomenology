# Slepian versus v0

Lemma 2 uses the *operator* χ_I P_τ χ_I on the
t-line, I = [0, γ1]. Its top eigenvalue is
1 − exp(−π τ |I|). We never computed its
eigenfunction (the first prolate).

v0 lives on the *cosine index* n = 0…N.
A DPSS of length 25 with NW∈[1,3] overlaps
v0 at most 0.39 — wrong space.

On the y-window [0, L], v0 is almost constant
(overlap 0.86) plus the first cosine. That is
the cheapest test function for a hole in t,
not the prolate supported on I.

What Slepian gives us: a number C=π in
ell ≤ π τ γ1 + log(1/A).
What it does not give us: a closed form for
v0, nor a transfer from Gram to Q.

To plot the actual prolate one would
discretise P_τ on I and take the top
eigenvector. That function is in t, not in n.
It is not needed for the bound we already use.
