# Gram λ0 at fixed μ, varying N (χ₂₉)

| μ | N | nz | λ0 |
|---|---|----|-----|
| 11 | 9 | 13 | 0.237 |
| 11 | 25 | 52 | 0.281 |
| 11 | 33 | 75 | 0.286 |
| 22 | 17 | 23 | 0.00307 |
| 22 | 37 | 63 | 0.00367 |
| 22 | 49 | 89 | 0.00379 |

λ0 *rises* a little when N grows (more zeros
in band). The drop 0.28→0.0037 is entirely
from L=log μ, not from dimension.

A-collapse is a window-length effect:
Slepian on a fixed I cannot track it, because
I=γ1 is independent of μ while the test
functions get more oscillations per unit t
(τ=L/2 grows). τ does grow, and λ₊ did go
0.61→0.80, but far too little. The missing
μ-dependence is in A(μ), i.e. in sampling
quality of Iᶜ as the bandwidth rises.
