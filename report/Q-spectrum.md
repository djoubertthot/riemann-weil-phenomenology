# Eigenvalues of Q — χ₂₉

From scan_s and the μ=11 diagonalisation.

| μ | λ0 | λ1 | λ1/λ0 | N_eff | bulk |
|---|-----|-----|-------|-------|------|
| 11 | 0.303 | 1.75 | 5.8 | 2.17 | 2.6 … 7.5 |
| 22 | 4.16e-3 | 1.21 | 292 | 1.68 | λ≥1, ell negative after i=1 |
| 38 | 6.96e-6 | 1.22e-1 | 1.8e4 | 1.69 | same |

λ0 falls exponentially with L=log μ
(desert / Slepian). λ1 stays O(1) at μ=11–22
then drops only to 0.12 at μ=38: the pair
peels off, the rest of the spectrum does not
collapse.

At μ=11 the ordered list is

    0.30, 1.75, 2.60, 2.86, 3.21, 3.38,
    3.82, 3.97, 4.04, 4.20, 4.37, 4.62,
    … 6.61, 6.85, 7.45

No other small eigenvalue. Isolation is
exactly one-dimensional (plus a mild partner
at 1.75). That is N_eff≈2.

Q is positive definite on these windows
(all λ>0). The Weil form on the cosine
basis does not need Gram to be declared
positive here; the matrix S already is.
