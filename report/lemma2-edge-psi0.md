## What is robust

−2 ln|ψ(0)| / ell ∈ [0.82, 0.98].
The budget of λ₀ is the edge, as
in §125 of the paper. χ₃ µ=80
(N_eff=3) is 0.98, not an outlier
on this ratio. Narrow deserts
(χ₁₃, χ₃₁) sit at 0.82–0.83: a
bit more of λ₀ lives off the
endpoint.

This ratio *is* the jackknife that
ell / τγ₁ failed.

## What is not

−2 ln|ψ(0)| / τγ₁ spans 1.81–6.18,
same factor three as ell / τγ₁.
Putting Lemma 2 on ψ(0) does not
create a constant C₀ against the
desert. It moves the inequality
to a simpler function:

\[
\bigl|\psi(0)\bigr|
\;\ge\;
\exp\bigl(-C_0\tau\gamma_1\bigr).
\]

Equivalent to the desert bound on
c_L once ell = −2 ln|ψ(0)| + O(1)
is granted (the table: O(1) is
0.02–0.18 of ell).

## Restated Lemma 2

Under RH, the ground-state edge of
Q_L satisfies

\[
-\ln\bigl|\psi(0)\bigr|
\;\le\;
C_0\,\tau\gamma_1 + C_1
\]

with C₀, C₁ absolute. The table
does not fix C₀. It does say that
proving this for ψ(0) proves it
for −ln λ₀, because the two
differ by a bounded relative
factor on every window we have.
