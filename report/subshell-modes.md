# Joint modes of the sub-shells

R=4 N=80. Top eigenvector of each F^{(n)}
versus the top mode v_Σ of the full compression.

Overlaps |⟨v_n, v_m⟩|: n=0,1,2,3 and inv share
one direction (0.78–0.97). n=4 is orthogonal
to all of them (mesh artefact: 2^4 h = 0.8).

(⟨v_Σ,v_0⟩=0.87 … ⟨v_Σ,v_3⟩=0.96).
