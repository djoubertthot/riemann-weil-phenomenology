# χ₅ stays two-mode when the basis grows

µ=16, prime-side Q, dps=22.

| dim | N_eff | k̄ | −ln λ₀ | a L² | v₀/|v₁| |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 9 | 2.13 | 0.53 | 27.17 | 20.59 | 1.044 |
| 17 | 2.14 | 0.54 | 33.11 | 20.91 | 1.034 |
| 25 | 2.14 | 0.54 | 33.65 | 20.91 | 1.034 |

Signs stay +−++−−−+. Extra hats after n=2
carry < 10⁻⁵ of the mass.

So off ζ, at this window:

- N_eff ≈ 2 is not a truncation artefact.
- a L² plateaus at 21, while −ln λ₀ still
  moves 27 → 34 then stalls. The curvature
  law a L² = −ln λ₀ is a ζ (four-mode) fact.
- Edge doubling stays at 2.15–2.16.
- Using −ln λ₀ in the 1/√2 form factor
  becomes dishonest once a L² ≠ −ln λ₀;
  the honest factor is exp(π²/(a L²)).

ζ had room for rungs n=2,3 because the
pole + even Γ_ℝ feed those hats. χ₅ does
not. The two-axis picture (arch n=1,
towers e₀) is the generic one.
