# Beurling D⁻ versus τ/π (χ₂₉)

Sampling of PW_τ needs D⁻(Λ) > τ/π.
Windows of relative length 0.15–1 on
[0,ω], [γ1,ω], [γ2,ω].

| μ | τ/π | D⁻[0,ω] | D⁻[γ1,ω] | D⁻[γ2,ω] |
|---|-----|---------|----------|----------|
| 11 | 0.382 | 0.424 | 0.546 | 0.579 |
| 22 | 0.492 | **0.456** | 0.560 | 0.589 |
| 38 | 0.579 | **0.526** | 0.539 | 0.660 |

After γ2, D⁻ stays above Nyquist: the bulk
samples. From 0, D⁻ falls *below* τ/π at
μ=22–38 — the left hole (desert + (γ1,γ2))
drags the infimum down as τ grows.

That is the same threshold where one-hole
Slepian stopped bounding ell. Beurling on
the whole ray sees the obstruction; Beurling
on Iᶜ minus the first gap does not.
