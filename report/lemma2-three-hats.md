    v = (0.711, −0.681, 0.179, 0.0032, −0.0027, −4.5×10⁻⁴, …)

Tail mass: 1.8×10⁻⁵.

Same shape on χ₅-38, χ₃-16, χ₄-16:
(+, −, +) in the first three,
then 10⁻³. Narrow deserts
(χ₁₃, χ₃₁) keep the mass on

is orthogonal to v (inner

## Rayleigh split (χ₅ µ=16)

    HH =  2.97×10⁻⁵
    HT = −2.97×10⁻⁵
    TH = −2.97×10⁻⁵
    TT =  2.97×10⁻⁵
    sum = 1.58×10⁻¹² = λ₀

Four terms of size 3×10⁻⁵ cancel
to 10⁻¹². λ₀ of the raw 3×3 is
2.3×10⁻⁶, a million times λ₀.
The tail is a counterterm: Schur
complement of the 3×3 block
inside S.

## Restated Lemma 2

Let S₃ be the top-left 3×3 of Q
and Δ the Schur complement
S/S₃. Then λ₀(Q) = λ_min(Δ)
(up to the 10⁻⁵ tail mass).
The exponential in ell is in Δ,
not in the 3-hat shape.

The shape itself is elementary:
the unique (up to scale) combination
of {1, cos θ, cos 2θ} that nearly
cancels at y=0. That cancellation
is why |ψ(0)| is small. Making it
*exponentially* small is the
coupling of those three hats to
n≥3 through the prime-side kernel.
