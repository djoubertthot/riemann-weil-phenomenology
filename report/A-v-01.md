## Reading

[0,1] will not fall to a
one-line comparison.
θ_v is an explicit mix of
1, cos(2π y/L), cos(4π y/L)
and the two sines — six
elementary functions.
A 3-point Gauss on [0,1]
(nodes ½ ± √(3/5)/2, ½)
is a finite arithmetic
check against those six,
not an inequality.

That is the remaining
hand computation: three
evaluations of θ_v and
of w(y)(2e^{-3y/2}−θ_v),
plus the known Gauss
weights 5/18, 8/18, 5/18
times length 1. The
singularity of w at 0
is integrable (w ~ 1/y)
and the first node sits
at 0.113, away from 0.
