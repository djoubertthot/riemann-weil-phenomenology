# Eigenvectors of Q and Gram — χ₂₉ μ=11

v0 (ground state), Q side, first coefficients:

    0.763, 0.586, −0.252, −0.070, −0.037, −0.016, −0.029, …

Gram: 0.767, 0.590, −0.241, …  (overlap 0.999)

## What the vector is

Mass on the constant and the first cosine
(n=0 and n=1). N_eff=2.17, k̄=0.53. That is
the two-bar mode: a DC offset plus one
oscillation on the window of length L=log 11.
Higher n are 10⁻² and do not carry the
eigenvalue.

## What it is not

Not a delta at a zero (that would be flat in n).
Not T_p·v0. Not a Maass form. The window
does not know γ; it only knows that a hole
at low t makes the smoothest test function
cheap.

## Mode 1

Also overlap 0.999, k̄≈0.7. The partner of
the pair. Together they exhaust the isolation
gap λ1/λ0≈5.8 at this small μ (the gap grows
at μ=22–38).

## Mode 6

k̄≈20, overlap 0.75. A high-frequency
direction where truncated zeros and the
prime tail disagree. Irrelevant to ell.
